⚡ HOW TO PLAY

🎯 The Goal

Two players compete across 16 school rooms. The Hacker tries to compromise as many rooms as possible, while the Cyber Controller defends and secures them. The player who controls the most rooms when the 10-minute timer runs out wins!

💀

🕹️ Controls

In each room, walk your character using Arrow keys or WASD. Look for the flashing ❗ beacon — that marks the challenge point. Walk up to it and press [E] or click it to start the challenge. Press [ESC] or click Leave Room to exit.

⏱️ Timer & Scoring

You have 10 minutes. Each room you control at the end counts as one point. Rooms switch colour immediately when a challenge is won — but there's a 30-second cooldown before that room can be contested again.
